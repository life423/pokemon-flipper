## Findings that explain the product failure

1. **Confirmed defect: raw research stops at one comparable, while the final buyer requires three.** `rawPricesWithSales` stops acquiring condition evidence once `MIN_MATCHED_SALES=1` is met. Final reseller selection requires three distinct matched exits within 30 days. Its Wait state does not request the missing two. A raw card can have `BUY_RAW` economics and never reach the buyer's Deal label. See [raw acquisition](../server/pricing/raw-comps.ts#L92), [price minimum](../shared/sold-evidence.ts#L8), [final liquidity gate](../server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts#L309) and [review research selection](../server/analysis/wait-skip-review.ts#L81).
2. **Confirmed defect: the AI hunt and buyer review disagree about eligibility.** Hunt inventory saves the permissive buyer-review reason but derives `assessable` from a stricter helper. Some missing-price rows are repairable by normal review but unavailable to the AI hunt. See [inventory](../server/analysis/deal-hunt-service.ts#L125), [buyer overrides](../shared/wait-skip-review.ts#L29) and [hunt rejection](../server/analysis/deal-hunt-service.ts#L190).
3. **Confirmed recovery gap: temporary browser failures can remain terminal Waits.** After the transport retry, an unavailable decision at the same review key has no automatic expiry/recovery. An explicit new search or changed evidence can reopen it, but merely waiting through the cooldown does not. See the buyer/UI catalogue below.
4. **Verified architecture gap: the other pricing APIs are mostly reference sources.** Executable resale paths primarily require exact PKMNPrices eBay transactions. Tracker, cited OpenAI research and Cardmarket generally populate guidance rather than a replacement executable price. Having several APIs configured does not currently give several interchangeable valuation routes. See [supplemental research](../server/pricing/supplemental-research.ts#L46) and [price provenance](../shared/money/price-evidence.ts#L59).
5. **Verified coverage limit: broad hunts do not consider all cards.** They seed from saved accepted evaluations, then truncate to eight grouped markets and ordinarily six live copies per market. Normal searches fetch 200 summaries per pass, optionally another lower-price pass, and retain 50. Two-miss rotation stays inside the supplied market menu. Increasing the API budget does not remove these limits.
6. **Verified grading restriction: a sleeve can remove executable grading economics.** A holder makes grading LIMITED, LIMITED forces low confidence, and current bounded rules move that numeric range to provisional display-only evidence. Raw resale may still qualify. This is a strict coded policy, not proof that every sleeved card is ungradable. See [holder rule](../server/analysis/rules.ts#L106), [confidence rule](../server/analysis/rules.ts#L164) and [range removal](../server/analysis/bounded-evaluate.ts#L1017).
7. **Verified catalog dependency, missed-deal impact not yet demonstrated:** native variants are built only from USD aggregate `prices` entries. An absent aggregate entry can prevent researching a sold market even if that endpoint has transactions. See [catalog conversion](../server/pricing/pkmnprices.ts#L266).
8. **Verified latency limitation:** rows still wait for preliminary screening, ranking and cleanup. Cold set searches can require many distinct requests under provider pacing. The saved 200-row Neo replay improved from 107.25 to 20.98 seconds with no provider requests; the separate live Neo metadata-only test still took 145.8 seconds. That test deliberately disabled automatic photos and is not evidence that the buyer's app setting was off or that a profitable card was missed.

## What the evidence says about the API prices

A SQL-only sample of 120 saved PKMNPrices sold pages contained 859 rows, all with numeric positive USD prices, exact attribution and parseable nonfuture dates. It included fixed-price sales, auctions, actual accepted offers and string half-grades. This sample does not support a general cents/dollars, currency, date or half-grade decoding bug. It does not establish that every source row is comparable or that every provider estimate is correct. The confirmed one-versus-three dead-end is a **use-of-evidence bug**, independent of whether the incoming price is correct.

Two independent synthetic replays reproduced it without providers. In one Alakazam fixture, five eligible recent Lightly Played transactions and supported prices yielded `BUY_RAW` with $41.34 projected profit. The first call acquired one accepted sold-condition reading; the second acquired none. Two buyer reviews both returned Wait, with zero evaluator recovery calls. Changing only accepted matched-condition evidence from one sale to three produced Buy. Lowering seller feedback below 10 or below 98% independently changed that control back to Wait. These are code controls, not available listings or realized profits.

### Material findings first

1. **Coverage limit: broad hunts do not search all Pokémon markets.** [server/analysis/same-set-markets.ts:221–255](../server/analysis/same-set-markets.ts#L221) builds them only from saved accepted evaluations. SQL starts with at most 1,200 identified evaluations saved in 30 days (`private-evaluation.ts:54–65`), selects 150 copies diversifying by card (`same-set-markets.ts:234–244`), plus at most 150 priced/photo-assessed diagnostic IDs across the latest retained searches (`search-diagnostic-store.ts:270–283`; retention constants 50 searches/30 days at17–18). There is no fresh full-catalog market generation here. A previously unresearched liquid card cannot enter the AI menu merely because its live listing is cheap.
2. **Coverage limit: menu and copy truncation happen before the planner chooses.** `deal-hunt-market-groups.ts:68,72–73` offers at most eight grouped markets. The ordinary hunt adds at most six cheapest eligible copies per market (`deal-hunt-service.ts:476–500`). The AI sees those six, not every retrieved candidate. Only standalone fresh raw grading gets a pool of 50 and AI review selection (`:480–485`). No continuation/cursor to copy seven or market nine exists in the planner menu.
3. **Coverage limit: normal search is at most one 200-summary page, optionally one cheaper 200-summary page, then 50 retained rows.** `find-deals.ts:146–157,249–255,282–317`. Three promising candidates suppress the extra cheaper page even if thousands remain. A zero-Deal result describes a bounded checked subset, not an exhaustive absence of bargains.
4. **Coverage limit: two-miss rotation exists, but only within the already supplied menu.** `deal-hunt.ts:275–287` explicitly makes the AI pick another unsearched market after two misses. Its different-card/year hints are real. It cannot create a new catalog market, request arbitrary code, revisit an already marked market, or retrieve a later source page. `:250–256,290–305` only offers unsearched indexed markets and marks one searched before discovering.
5. **Defect: saved hunt inventory has two different eligibility decisions for the same row.** `deal-hunt-service.ts:125` saves `block = buyerReviewBlock(source)`, but `:126` saves `assessable` from the stricter `autoAnalysisBlockReason`. [shared/wait-skip-review.ts:29–33](../shared/wait-skip-review.ts#L29) intentionally reopens missing/deferred sold evidence, raw premium, no-candidate and some dropped rows for the buyer review path; `deal-hunt-service.ts:190` refuses an unassessed hunt row with `assessable=false`. The general buyer review can therefore repair a row the AI hunt cannot select. Fix suggestion: derive hunt `assessable` from the same buyer-review eligibility contract, preserving hard exclusions and exact-slab rejection.
6. **Coverage limit: collector-number fallback may not run after a photo-based miss.** `same-set-listings.ts:216` stops after the full-name query if any new preliminarily usable candidate exists. The later photo check can reject those candidates; the already-searched market has no menu action to then run its number aliases. Fix suggestion: expose query/copy continuation after actual failed verification, not just after zero preliminary hits.
7. **Coverage limit: an eight-check planner is separate from API headroom.** `deal-hunt.ts:50–52,221–222,250–264` bounds the plan at eight actions, or 11 collector-auction /12 reseller-auction actions once candidates exist. A market search consumes a step. Browsing another market requires more than three collector/four other steps left so a copy can be verified. Higher API or OpenAI budgets alone do not remove this hard planner limit.

### Verified integration defect: raw producer stops at one, final resale consumer requires three

The raw price producer accepts one exact-condition physically reviewed sale ([shared/sold-evidence.ts:8](../shared/sold-evidence.ts#L8)). It acquires another sold-photo reading only while its current matched count is below that one-sale minimum ([server/pricing/raw-comps.ts:92-105](../server/pricing/raw-comps.ts#L92)). The acquirer itself permits one attempt ([server/ai/sold-condition.ts:141-143,221-225](../server/ai/sold-condition.ts#L141)). Once a market has one accepted same-condition sale, later normal evaluations see a sufficient price and do not acquire additional copies.

The final resale decision counts only physically condition-matched, linked, confirmed sales in that exact condition and market ([server/analysis/same-set-markets.ts:133-157](../server/analysis/same-set-markets.ts#L133)). It requires at least three within 30 days (`:88,156-161`; [server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts:309-316](../server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts#L309)). Seller-labelled transactions and Tracker/web references do not increase that count. Thus a new raw market can become stuck at one supported price observation and never satisfy automatic resale liquidity, even with many matching seller-condition transactions. Grading/slab routes or already-established shared markets can still qualify independently; this does not mean every raw card is globally impossible.

Pure offline replay used a synthetic Dark Gengar, exact Unlimited Holo Neo Destiny 6/105, 100 recent confirmed USD seller-LP transactions, $60 purchase and $200 sale estimate, no graded route, fresh active terms and zero profit/ROI minimums. Only the number of already accepted physical LP readings changed. All assertions passed; no provider or AI was called.

| Accepted physical LP readings initially cached | New acquisition calls | Final executable LP sale count | Underwriting | Final resale decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 0 | 1 | 1 | BUY_RAW, $99.27 profit | WAIT: fewer than 3 recent sales |
| 1 | 0 | 1 | BUY_RAW, $99.27 profit | WAIT: fewer than 3 recent sales |
| 3 | 0 | 3 | BUY_RAW, $99.27 profit | BUY |

This proves a recovery mismatch between independently reasonable rules. It is not evidence that an actual current Dark Gengar listing offers that profit. The exact recorded WAIT reason was: `Sell raw projects $99.27 after purchase and selling costs. 1 matched Lightly Played raw sale in the last 30 days. The resale hunt needs at least 3 to support buying.` A fix should connect required exit liquidity to bounded evidence acquisition, or explicitly distinguish sale activity from stricter condition-price evidence, while preserving identity, amount and condition correctness.

## Things that are not universal Deal vetoes

- Low feedback under 25, less than 15% room under the maximum price, sparse/old-looking ratings and a losing downside can lower the rating without independently killing every Deal ([shared/money/rating.ts:79](../shared/money/rating.ts#L79)). The final buyer path separately hard-vetoes known feedback below 10 or below 98%, and grading scenarios have stronger downside rules.
- A missing certificate or scratched holder is not automatically a counterfeit/cracked-holder rejection ([server/identity/slab-check.ts:110](../server/identity/slab-check.ts#L110)). Exact resale/collector gates still distinguish intact and scratched cases.
- A sleeve, missing close-up, or provisional grade does not universally prohibit raw resale; it can disable the numeric grading route described above.
- One failed alternative pricing route does not invalidate another fully supported profitable route ([shared/money/underwriting.ts:727](../shared/money/underwriting.ts#L727)).
- First fifteen is a priority order, not the automatic review limit. Six concurrent slots limit throughput, not total eligible retained-card coverage.
- Provider totals and broad raw/graded sales activity do not equal three distinct condition/grade-matched exits. Liquidity requirements are separate from the one-sale minimum for a price estimate.

### Findings that explain a profitable card still not becoming a Deal

1. **Reachable raw-liquidity dead end.** [shared/sold-evidence.ts:8](../shared/sold-evidence.ts#L8) defines `MIN_MATCHED_SALES=1`. [server/pricing/raw-comps.ts:92](../server/pricing/raw-comps.ts#L92) counts accepted exact-condition evidence, and its only new sold-copy acquisition is conditional on `matched < MIN_MATCHED_SALES` at :94–105. Therefore an ordinary fresh market acquires at most one matching copy, and a replay with that copy cached acquires none. Final buyer review calls the resale-hunt checker through [server/analysis/wait-skip-review.ts:94–97,174–175](../server/analysis/wait-skip-review.ts#L94), whose own hard threshold is three recent exact-condition exits ([server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts:309–317](../server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts#L309); constant [server/analysis/same-set-markets.ts:88](../server/analysis/same-set-markets.ts#L88)). `nextCheck` at `wait-skip-review.ts:81–84` sees current prices, identity and terms, so it does not ask for extra sold evidence just because the final result lacks liquidity. Re-running the same review does not repair this.

   Offline reachable proof with provider calls disabled: Synthetic **Alakazam**, five eligible recent LP sales: first pricing acquires one; second pricing still has one, total acquisitions one. Underwriting is `BUY_RAW`, net profit **$41.34**; buyer reviewer with `allowResearch:true` returns `WAIT` on two calls and invokes the evaluator **zero** times. Exact reason: one recent matched LP sale, at least three required. Adding only the other two accepted observations changes the same copy to `BUY`. This demonstrates a missing research continuation, not evidence that seller-described transactions should automatically be treated as verified condition matches.

2. **Seller feedback is a hard final veto, rather than a warning or AI judgment.** [server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts:325–329](../server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts#L325) converts every otherwise supported Buy to Wait if the supplied feedback score is below **10**, or supplied feedback percentage below **98**. Missing seller data does not trigger this veto. The control above becomes `BUY` with three accepted observations; changing only seller score to 9 (100% feedback) or feedback percentage to 97.9 (10,000 ratings) changes it to `WAIT`. This can intentionally remove some inexperienced/non-specialist sellers from the user's desired strategy. It is a product-policy choice, not a pricing error.

3. **Transient unavailable checks latch as completed Waits in the browser.** [client/src/search/useBuyerReviews.ts:67](../client/src/search/useBuyerReviews.ts#L67) removes every row with any completed decision from pending, including `unavailable`. Thrown failures are reduced to a generic same-key `WAIT/unavailable` at :104–107, without retry time or a quote lifetime. Returned server `WAIT/unavailable` results are stored at :103 in the same way; server cooldown metadata is not part of `BuyerListingReview` ([shared/wait-skip-review.ts:10–20](../shared/wait-skip-review.ts#L10)). `recheckQuote` at :118–122 only admits an expired `validUntil`; these failures have none. The server's actual retry-after receipt at [server/analysis/wait-skip-review.ts:352–359](../server/analysis/wait-skip-review.ts#L352) can expire, yet the browser does not call again. Request transport gets exactly one retry for retryable HTTP/network errors ([client/src/api.ts:70–79](../client/src/api.ts#L70)), not an ongoing repair loop. A new explicit search or changed key can reopen the work.

   Actual-source offline headless-browser proof with a neutral Alakazam fixture, all APIs intercepted. HTTP503 caused two calls (one retry), then remained Wait after four seconds. HTTP200 `WAIT/unavailable:true` with an explicit three-second cooldown caused one call and remained Wait after four seconds. Provider calls **0**, browser errors **0**. The provider would be reachable again, but the unchanged row has no subsequent dispatch.

4. **The numbers and group names count different stages.** The header's `buyer decisions ready` counts all retained rows with completed non-unavailable decisions, whereas `All matching` contains only current filtered rows. Queue includes every retained owned row ([client/src/App.tsx:940–944](../client/src/App.tsx#L940)), not only visible rows. This makes a finished/100% search with few ready decisions possible. It is a visibility/diagnostic explanation, not necessarily proof of a lost profitable copy.

5. **Paid evidence can be complete while the separate final decision is waiting.** `groupSearchResults` ([client/src/search/results.ts:95–100](../client/src/search/results.ts#L95)) puts queued rows with no final buyer decision in Sorting, and any explicit `WAIT` or `SKIP` in Wait or skip even if a pre-existing underwriting path says Buy. This is deliberate precedence. The specific reason for the final veto must be surfaced to distinguish liquidity, seller policy, stale terms, missing evidence, and temporary failure.

Auctions then need the separate as-listed below-market opportunity, not just grading upside (:168–172). Fixed-price passes through final hunt-policy checker (:174–175), including liquidity and seller policy.
- Identity not pricing-ready => Wait, while preserving seller claim and scoped reference explanation (:177–178).
- Slab likely fake => Skip; label/holder not OK => Wait; thin or reference-only grade value => Wait (:179–184).
- Possible raw authenticity concern or suspected crease => Wait (:186–187).
- Unpriced/deferred raw condition route => Wait or conservative raw guidance; no exact assessed-condition sold match => Wait even if a different condition has a price (:188–199).
- Unknown raw condition / insufficient surface or edges => Wait (:201–205).

`nextCheck` (:66–84) can proactively assess photos, resolve set-specific frame/stamp or missing back, perform deeper inspection, obtain missing/current prices, or refresh terms. It deliberately stops on an ended/wrong/fake/new-concern copy, current complete priced Pass, possible counterfeit/likely fake slab, or a gap for which no additional listed photos exist. It currently lacks an action triggered by insufficient **count** of recent final exit sales.

### Browser scheduling, cancellation, and result acceptance gates

`client/src/App.tsx` and `client/src/search/useBuyerReviews.ts`:

- Auto setting Off (`autoSetting===0`) disables automatic final reviews (:935–936). Defaults are unlimited; legacy saved 3/25 caps migrate to unlimited ([client/src/search/settings.ts:22–25,45–52](../client/src/search/settings.ts#L22)). Current choices are unlimited or Off.
- Every retained row with a discovery owner enters the final queue, priority IDs first (`App.tsx:940–944`). Fifteen is a priority set, not a final-review cap. It does not silently skip the other 35 rows.
- Must have completed source run, settled target input debounce (500ms, :297–303), searchStatus done, no explicit price refresh underway (:958–961). Source screening therefore finishes before final buyer-review dispatch. No paid photo dispatch from provisional discovery rows is allowed.
- Stopped search or changed submitted format/kind/min/max control disables automatic reviews (:228–233,958). A fresh search resets this scope; changing filters does not broaden an already completed source result (:726–773).
- `allowResearch` is false when global analysis is blocked or explicitly paused (:959). Existing evidence can still return a final decision through server cache-only behavior.
- Concurrency is **six** shared buyer-review/manual-analysis/photo-review slots ([shared/research-policy.ts:16](../shared/research-policy.ts#L16), hook :79–85). Busy copy, already running copy, missing discovery owner, or identical completed/transition key => no new dispatch. Completed responses fill slots independently.
- New search, different target/offer/research/ready/enabled state, or component unmount aborts old controllers (hook :45–50). Response whose old search/key no longer matches is ignored (:91–94). This prevents stale Buy promotion.
- Response must match listing ID/evaluation ID, have supported decision and nonblank reason; unavailable cannot be Buy or offer (:95–96). Buy or offer must have a valid unexpired terms lifetime; Buy also requires Buy underwriting (:97–99). Offer must be enabled, positive, no larger than its supported ceiling (:100–101).
- App checks current title/gallery; changed photo evidence cannot support Buy/Offer (:966–969). Refreshed price/shipping must agree and availability must be ACTIVE (:971–973). Failure produces unavailable Wait in the hook.
- Accepted listing updates clear old screening/mismatch notes ([shared/fresh-listing.ts:6–17](../shared/fresh-listing.ts#L6)). `withResolvedCriteria` also repairs a compatible `UNCONFIRMED_CRITERIA` using evaluated identity ([shared/search-criteria.ts:167–169](../shared/search-criteria.ts#L167), called `App.tsx:307–314`). No proven permanent hidden-unconfirmed-card bug was found on this path.
- Buy/offer quotes expire after **120 seconds**, separate from six-hour resale freshness ([shared/search-diagnostic.ts:52](../shared/search-diagnostic.ts#L52), hook :97). The hook converts expired quote to Wait (:62–64). Auction expiration or newer auction-terms event also converts to Wait (:57–60). Only expired quote results have a per-card `recheckQuote` path (:118–122).
- Stop aborts source/manual operations, pauses new work, and `enabled:false` effect aborts buyer-review requests (`App.tsx:870–883`, hook :45–50). Accepted historical answers can remain visible without continuing paid stages.
- Old automatic-analysis path is used only without a review selection/completed run; it also has candidate admission, current page, outcome-based concurrency, budget, stopped/paused/error and existing/busy guards (`App.tsx:890–911`). Supported new final-review path supersedes this loop.

Global UI block codes are `AI_CREDITS`, `AI_PAUSED`, `AI_BUDGET`, `STORAGE_UNAVAILABLE`, `PRICE_RESEARCH_BUDGET`, `PRICE_RESEARCH_UNAVAILABLE` ([client/src/search/presentation.ts:5–20](../client/src/search/presentation.ts#L5); handled `App.tsx:560–575`). Credit/storage/accounting outages can stop the source stream and other pending stages; budget admission leaves unrelated accepted work and metadata discovery running. The Retry notice only exists for recoverable configured classes; it retains the same search/account allowance (`App.tsx:1394–1401`), rather than granting a new budget.

### Every browser filter and classification that can keep a row out of Deals

[client/src/search/results.ts:61–80](../client/src/search/results.ts#L61) filters the source rows before *all* tabs:

1. Any remaining listing `match`, screening junk, evaluated wrong-card identification (:68).

After filtering, `groupSearchResults` (:83–107) applies this precedence:

- Any other final Wait or Skip enters **Wait or skip** (:99), overriding old positive economics.

[shared/analysis.ts:69–127](../shared/analysis.ts#L69) base grouping gates:

- Obscured disclosed wear priced Pass => no Deal (:72).

- Priced `PASS` remains outside Deals; unresolved identity/slab/authenticity/deeper concern => review; realistic enabled offer => Offers; otherwise no special group (:99–108).

### Collector, auction and standalone hunts are intentionally separate outputs

- Collector Buy means delivered price at or below matching slab value with verified holder, not necessarily profit after resale fees; [server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts:269–277](../server/analysis/deal-hunt-evidence.ts#L269). A fair collector Buy may correctly remain outside the resale Deals tab. [client/src/search/hunt-result.ts:18–20](../client/src/search/hunt-result.ts#L18) gives Collector bid a distinct label. Ordinary buyer review always requests the resale interpretation (:94–97 of reviewer), regardless of the separately selected collector hunt.
- Auctions produce Bid opportunity; server buyer-review ordinary `decision` remains Wait with typed `auctionOpportunity` (`wait-skip-review.ts:262–263`), and UI puts it in Bids, not Deals. Both final auction terms freshness and strict less-than-ten-hours eligibility apply. Root's auction audit covers the economic checks.
- A standalone hunt renders returned destination card and its discovered other candidates in the hunt panel; these need not become ordinary retained search cards/Deals ([client/src/components/DealHunt.tsx:52–83,119–159](../client/src/components/DealHunt.tsx#L52)). It currently displays listing cards when the hunt completes (:190); a busy hunt without rows is not proof no marketplace candidates were found.
- Hunt returned identity must match original run, selected goal, allowed decision/format, retained IDs or permitted alternative scope, validated URLs/title/gallery, fresh auction/grading quote ([client/src/search/hunt-result.ts:32–64](../client/src/search/hunt-result.ts#L32)).
- App rejects stale hunt run/scope, wrong alternative scope, wrong same-set expansion, non-English copy, busy copy, changed title/gallery/terms, newer existing assessment, expired grading or bid, non-ACTIVE terms, or current UI filter mismatch (`App.tsx:1123–1157`). No side-panel evidence is opened/promoted on failed acceptance.
- Hunt choices restrict cross-card support to resale, BIN raw grading, and auction collector slab (`hunt-result.ts:23–25`, DealHunt.tsx:181–182). Selected goal and format can therefore rule out a tool path before any candidate economics.
- Collector binder/artwork panels add their own requirements (exact current photo evidence, no authenticity/crease concern, clear front/back, comparable artwork/copy and cheaper delivered price) in [client/src/search/collector-finds.ts:56–110,115–139](../client/src/search/collector-finds.ts#L56). They are optional collector value panels, not blockers on core Deals.

### Diagnostic observability limitation

[client/src/search/diagnostic.ts:25–36](../client/src/search/diagnostic.ts#L25) collapses all final decisions to `BUYER_WAIT`, `BUYER_SKIP` or `BUYER_CHECK_UNAVAILABLE`; the detailed `BuyerListingReview.reason` is not retained in `SearchDiagnosticRow` ([shared/search-diagnostic.ts:20–27](../shared/search-diagnostic.ts#L20)). Therefore a search summary alone cannot tell whether a profitable copy was stopped by insufficient recent sale count, seller veto, transport failure, stale quote, or genuinely missing photos. The code's per-copy returned reason exists in UI/private receipts, but the aggregate audit loses that causal specificity. This is an instrumentation gap relevant to the request to identify every cause, rather than a direct price gate.

Existing distinct diagnostic codes include `AUTO_OFF`, `AUTO_LIMIT`, `NOT_ASSESSED`, `FILTERS_CHANGED`, all provider/accounting blocks, `AI_EVIDENCE_HELD`, `PHOTO_EVIDENCE_INCONSISTENT`, `ASSESSMENT_FAILED`, `SHIPPING_UNCONFIRMED`, `PRICING_DEFERRED`, `IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`, `SLAB_LABEL_UNRESOLVED`, `MATERIAL_REVIEW`, and `PRICE_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`; full codebook is [shared/search-diagnostic.ts:8–18](../shared/search-diagnostic.ts#L8). App chooses generic waiting reason at :1165–1168.
