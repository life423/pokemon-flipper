## Verified behavior

- Current bid and maximum item/snipe bid are separately labeled. A stated
  winning-price scenario supplies its own profit, return and total investment.
  Above-limit auctions show the outcome at the current bid and say to pass.
- Any/Any targets reprice locally and label the limit as a break-even ceiling.
  Low confidence/unknown likely grade marks a ceiling provisional. Unknown
  pricing produces no invented maximum. Ended auctions have an ended state.
- Closing guides reuse raw condition quotes or dated slab sales, with observed
  range/sample size for the latter. Future, stale or undated slab sales are
  excluded. A graded resale forecast is never a raw closing guide. Unknown raw
  condition or weak photos leave the guide unavailable.
- Both PSA and CGC show the shared photo range, separate resale estimates and
  separate grading costs/limits. The selected route is identified. Slabs retain
  their certified company/grade. Missing grading prices stay unavailable.
- Wrapping tabs, the type/spacing scale and wider cards remain readable at
  phone/tablet/desktop sizes. Opening analysis leaves result widths unchanged.
  Modal analysis uses one scrolling area; its gallery has no horizontal strip.
- Tab/Shift+Tab wrap inside analysis, Escape closes it, focus returns to the
  opening card, and background scrolling unlocks. Settings remain accessible
  and targets are editable on phones. Gallery next/previous updates the photo.
